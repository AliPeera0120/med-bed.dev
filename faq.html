FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is MedBed?

A secure container that keeps a patient’s medication locked to them as they move through a hospital, and opens in seconds for authorized staff.

Who can open it?

Only authorized staff, using the credentials they already carry.

What can it hold?

Everyday medication plus dedicated, protected slots for items like insulin pens.

How does it attach?

It mounts securely to a hospital bed, wheelchair, or gurney and travels with the patient.

How much does it cost?

MedBed is designed to be low-cost enough to deploy facility-wide, and to pay for itself in under two shifts of a nurse’s time. Contact us for pricing.
