The solution

MedBed keeps medication locked to the patient — wherever they go.

A secure container that mounts to the bed or chair, opens in seconds for authorized staff, and travels with the patient through every transfer.

Request a Demo

What MedBed does

Secure, staff-only access

Opens only for authorized staff, using the credentials they already carry. No keys to lose.

Locks automatically

Secures itself again after every use, so medication is never left exposed.

Travels with the patient

Mounts firmly to a bed, wheelchair, or gurney and moves through the whole facility.

Purpose-built compartments

Organized space for everyday medication, with dedicated protected slots for insulin pens.

Impossible to miss

Brightly finished and clearly labeled so it’s never left behind or confused with anything else.

Built for real hospitals

A rugged, easy-to-handle body engineered for constant transport and cleaning.

How it fits

Four simple steps in the flow of care.

1

Assign

MedBed is assigned to a patient and loaded with their medication.

2

Travel

It stays locked and moves with the patient to their new location.

3

Access

Authorized staff open it in seconds to administer medication.

4

Secure

It locks again automatically, ready for the next move.
