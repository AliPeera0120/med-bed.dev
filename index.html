The solution

One simple change: the medication travels with the patient.

When a patient moves between units, their medication usually doesn’t move with them — so it’s discarded and re-ordered. MedBed fixes that. It locks each patient’s medication to their bed or chair, opens in seconds for authorized staff, and secures itself again automatically.

Secure, staff-only access

Only authorized staff can open it, with the badge they already carry.

Travels with the patient

Mounts to the bed, wheelchair, or gurney and moves with them.

Purpose-built inside

Organized compartments keep doses — including insulin pens — safe and accounted for.
