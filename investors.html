The opportunity

An addressable slice of a massive problem

U.S. health care wastes an estimated 25% of what it spends — hundreds of billions of dollars a year [1]. Medication is one of the clearest, most addressable pieces of that waste [2]. MedBed targets it directly with a device every hospital can afford to deploy at scale.
